Building in Solana Beach

Solana Beach mixes older beach-adjacent homes with newer construction, often on compact lots where every square foot of indoor and outdoor space is doing work.

Small footprints reward careful planning more than large ones. A kitchen that divides awkwardly into standard cabinet widths, a bathroom where the door swing collides with the vanity, a yard where a patio cover has to shade the right spot without darkening the room behind it — these are the problems we spend most of our time on here.

Coastal exposure applies throughout. Salt air is hard on metal, and the daily cycle of damp marine mornings and dry afternoons is harder on timber than steady conditions in either direction. It shapes what we specify for decks, patio covers and exterior work.

Outdoor space is used most of the year in Solana Beach, which is why decks, covers and hardscape are worth building to the same standard as the interior.
