Our Story

Residential work done the right way

Guild Builders was founded in 2021 as a family-owned company committed to reliable, high-quality craftsmanship, clear communication, and professional service.

Every project is approached with attention to detail, strong project management, and respect for your budget and timeline.

Whether it’s a renovation, new build, or structural improvement, our goal is simple: do the work right and make the process smooth for you.

I started out working alongside my dad, who was a plumber. After three years in the army I came back to construction, and in 2021 I started Guild Builders here in San Diego. It is still a family run company, and you deal with me directly rather than a rotating cast of project managers, so you always know what is happening and what comes next, and the schedule is managed rather than hoped for.

Every kitchen and bathroom on this site is work we delivered ourselves, from the first layout decision through to the last piece of hardware. That is where the advice in our articles comes from: layouts that worked once people cooked in them, materials that held up, and the things that turn up once a wall is open. We are licensed in California and rated 5.0 on Google, Facebook and Thumbtack.

Your home is somewhere people still live while the work is going on, and it gets treated that way. I would rather tell you something inconvenient early than let it surprise you later.

A kitchen looks its best the week it is finished. Ours are built so it still looks that way once it has been cooked in, cleaned around and lived with. That means doors that close square, grout that stays tight, and finishes chosen to take daily use.
