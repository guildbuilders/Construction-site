How We Work

How We Deliver An Incredible San Diego Remodeling Experience

  • Design Comes First.

    We plan layout, cabinetry, countertops, tile and hardware as one design, so you see the finished kitchen and approve it on paper before any demolition starts.

  • Every Trade Under One Roof.

    Cabinetry, counters, tile, plumbing, electrical and flooring are all scheduled and managed by us, so you are never left chasing subcontractors or refereeing between them.

  • Planned To The Day And The Dollar.

    Your estimate lays out materials, labor and a realistic timeline in plain language, and if something moves we tell you early rather than letting it surprise you halfway through.

  • Built To Hold Its Value.

    The kitchen carries more weight than any other room when a house is valued. We build with layouts and finishes that keep working long after the job is signed off.

Our Process

How It Works

1

Free Consultation

We come to your home at no cost, measure the space, and talk through how you actually use your kitchen day to day. You get design ideas and a good sense of what your remodel involves.

2

Clear Estimate

You receive a clear, detailed estimate that lays out materials, labor, and a realistic timeline in plain language. No vague ballpark numbers, and no surprise line items appearing halfway through the job.

3

Your Design

We design it with you, not at you. Layout, cabinetry, countertops, tile, and hardware get chosen together, and nothing is final until you say it is. You see the finished kitchen and change your mind on paper rather than halfway through the build.

4

We Build It

Our team manages every trade your remodel needs, so you only ever deal with us. We protect your home, clean the site at the end of each day, and keep you updated at every stage until the final detail is finished.

Your Guild Builders Guarantee

Our reputation rests on two things: the standard of the work, and doing what we said we would do.

Guarantee #1:

No surprise change orders.

The price you sign is the price you pay. If you ask us to take on work beyond the agreed scope, or we open a wall and find something that has to be put right, you see the cost in writing and approve it before we touch it. Nothing is ever added to your invoice after the fact.

Guarantee #2:

We finish what we start.

We do not leave your home until you and our team are 100% satisfied with the finished result. Put another way, we do not treat your remodel any differently from the ones we have done in our own homes. The punch list gets closed out before we call the job finished, not after.

Guarantee #3:

Our mistakes are on us.

We plan your remodel down to the dollar and the day, and we hold ourselves to it. If our own workmanship, ordering, or scheduling costs you time or money, we absorb it rather than passing it on. Your price moves only when you approve a change in writing first.
