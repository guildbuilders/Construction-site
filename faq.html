Good to Know

Working With Us

What areas do you serve?

We provide residential construction and remodeling throughout San Diego County and North County, including kitchens, bathrooms, full home renovations, decks, patio covers, and exterior improvements.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Guild Builders Inc. is fully licensed and bonded (California License #1154614), so your project is handled by qualified, accountable professionals.

What types of projects do you handle?

We handle kitchens and cabinets, bathrooms, full home renovations, decks and patio covers, hardscape, and exterior renovations — from single-room remodels to whole-home projects.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Reach out through our contact page or request a quote, and we'll discuss your project and provide a clear, honest estimate.

Do you help with design and material selection?

Absolutely. We help with layout, materials, finishes, and functionality so the finished space is beautiful, durable, and suited to how you live.

How long does a typical remodel take?

It depends on the scope. A bathroom might take a few weeks while a full home renovation takes longer. We provide a realistic timeline up front and keep you updated throughout.

How do we get started?

Simple — contact us with a few details about your project. We'll set up a consultation, assess the space, and provide an estimate and timeline.
