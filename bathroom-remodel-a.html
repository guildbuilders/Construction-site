Your Guild Builders Guarantee

Our reputation rests on two things: the standard of the work, and doing what we said we would do.

Guarantee #1:

No surprise change orders.

The price you sign is the price you pay. If you ask us to take on work beyond the agreed scope, or we open a wall and find something that has to be put right, you see the cost in writing and approve it before we touch it. Nothing is ever added to your invoice after the fact.

Guarantee #2:

We finish what we start.

We do not leave your home until you and our team are 100% satisfied with the finished result. Put another way, we do not treat your remodel any differently from the ones we have done in our own homes. The punch list gets closed out before we call the job finished, not after.

Guarantee #3:

Our mistakes are on us.

We plan your remodel down to the dollar and the day, and we hold ourselves to it. If our own workmanship, ordering, or scheduling costs you time or money, we absorb it rather than passing it on. Your price moves only when you approve a change in writing first.

Questions San Diego Homeowners Ask

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to commit to a full remodel to talk to you?

No. The first visit is a free in-home estimate and design consultation, and there is nothing owed if you decide not to go ahead. Plenty of people use it to find out what their bathroom would actually cost before they commit to anything.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

It depends on the scope. A tub to shower conversion runs a couple of weeks; a full bathroom with layout or plumbing changes takes longer. You get a realistic timeline in the estimate rather than an optimistic one, and we keep you updated as the work goes.

Do you help with design and material selection?

Yes, and it happens before anything is torn out. We work through layout, tile, vanity, fixtures and lighting with you as one design, so the finished bathroom is decided on paper while changes are still free.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Guild Builders Inc. holds California contractor license #1154614. You can look it up on the CSLB website, and the number appears on every estimate and contract we send.

What areas do you serve?

All of San Diego County, including La Jolla, Del Mar, Solana Beach, Rancho Santa Fe, Carmel Valley, Encinitas, Carlsbad, Vista, Poway, San Marcos, Escondido, Lakeside, Fallbrook, La Mesa, El Cajon, Chula Vista and Bonita.
