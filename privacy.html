Privacy Policy

How we handle information you share with us through this website.

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Guild Builders Inc. ("Guild Builders," "we," "us") operates guildbuildersgroup.com. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We have tried to describe our actual practices plainly rather than in the broadest terms possible.

Information you give us

We collect information only when you choose to send it. Our forms ask for:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), and whatever you write in the message field.
  • Estimate request forms: your name, phone number, project type, and ZIP code.

You may also contact us directly by phone or email, in which case we hold whatever you choose to tell us.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ours records some technical information when you visit. This includes your approximate location (typically city level, derived from your IP address), the type of device and browser you are using, which pages you viewed and for how long, and the website or advertisement that referred you.

This is collected through Google Analytics and Google Ads, described below.

How we use this information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project
  • To prepare estimates and, if you engage us, to carry out the work
  • To understand which pages and advertisements bring people to the site, so we can improve both
  • To keep records of our business communications

We do not add you to a marketing mailing list from a form submission, and we do not send promotional email campaigns from the information collected here.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We do rely on a small number of service providers to operate the website. They handle information on our behalf, for the purposes described here:

  • FormSubmit — processes submissions from our forms and delivers them to us by email.
  • Google — provides the analytics and advertising measurement described below.
  • Cloudflare — hosts and serves this website.

We may also disclose information where we are required to do so by law, or where it is necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, and the Google Ads tag to measure the results of our advertising — for example, whether someone who clicked an advertisement went on to submit a form or tap our phone number.

These services set cookies and similar identifiers in your browser. They tell us about patterns of behaviour on the site; they do not tell us who you are by name.

You can limit this if you prefer:

  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings.
  • Google provides an opt-out add-on for Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • You can manage advertising personalisation at myadcenter.google.com.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and project records for as long as needed to respond to you, to carry out and stand behind work we have performed, and to meet our record-keeping obligations. If you would like us to delete your information and we are not required to keep it, contact us and we will do so.

Your choices

  • You can ask what information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct it if it is wrong.
  • You can ask us to delete it, subject to records we are required to retain.
  • You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

California residents have specific rights under California privacy law, including the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. As stated above, we do not sell personal information. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

Security

This site is served over an encrypted connection, and we limit access to enquiry information to the people who need it to respond to you. No method of transmitting information over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is intended for adults arranging construction and remodeling work. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

Our site may link to websites we do not operate. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and revise the date at the top.
